How the calibrated cursor works

The cursor is now driven by an One-Euro filter — a low-pass filter whose cutoff frequency rises with velocity. When your hand is still it smooths aggressively; when you move quickly it barely filters, so there's almost no lag. This is the same trick VR headsets use to keep controllers feeling 1:1.

Calibration maps four raw fingertip positions (recorded at the screen corners) onto the screen rectangle using an affine transform. That means the calibration handles slight hand rotation or camera tilt, not just scale and offset. The mapping is stored in localStorage and reused on every visit.

Mis-clicks are eliminated three ways: pinch must be confirmed for 3 consecutive frames, a click has a 300 ms cooldown so fluttering pinches don't double-fire, and a "fist" hand (tiny bounding box) can't trigger pinch recognition at all.
