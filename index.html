Self-tuning cursor

The Auto-tuner AI watches your cursor in real time. Every 500 ms it computes three scores — jitter (RMS of sub-pixel movement when your hand is nearly still), lag (time for the cursor to catch up when you stop), and overshoot (how far past the target the cursor lands after a fast move). Every 3 s it nudges one parameter toward the target zone.

Move a slider manually and that parameter becomes locked — the AI stops touching it and shows a purple "locked" badge next to its label. Tap Reset to release all locks and let the AI start fresh.
