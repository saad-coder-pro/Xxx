Selectable Text — try long-pinch

How selection works

Point your index finger at the start of the sentence you want to select. Then pinch your thumb and index finger together and hold for about half a second. The cursor changes to a hollow ring — that means the selection anchor has been placed at that character.

Now move your hand. Whatever the cursor passes over gets added to the selection — forwards, backwards, up, down, across paragraphs. Release the pinch to finish, and the selected text stays highlighted just like a normal mouse drag. It uses the browser's native caretRangeFromPoint API, so it works on any selectable text on any page, including <code> spans like this one.

The virtual cursor also drives the real mouse: as soon as you move your physical mouse, the white dot follows it natively. Move your hand again and it snaps back to fingertip tracking. Both inputs share the same click, drag and select behaviours.
