AB-filter + affine calibration

The pointer is now driven by an alpha-beta filter — a steady-state Kalman tuned for cursor tracking. Position is passed through with almost no smoothing; the filter only smooths velocity. The DOM cursor is placed at lastPos + velocity × (elapsed + pipelineLatency), which cancels the camera + inference delay. A tiny dead-zone removes jitter without adding lag.

Calibration records your hand's raw camera coordinates at four screen corners and computes a full affine transform — so a camera placed off to the left maps correctly to your screen. Stored in localStorage.
